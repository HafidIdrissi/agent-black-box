# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique the work rather than the person. Welcome people regardless of background, experience, identity, disability, or language.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure people to work without consent or post repeated promotional messages.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, or limit participation. Explain moderation decisions when doing so will not expose private information or worsen harm.

Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools for harassment or private-information disclosure. For other conduct concerns, use a private contact channel a maintainer explicitly lists on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive incident details in an issue. This project does not currently provide a dedicated confidential reporting address.
